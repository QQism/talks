# Accessible travel

- Prefer places with `step_free: true` from `find_attractions` and `find_restaurants`. Strollers and wheelchairs need the same thing.
- Warn about places that are only partly step-free, such as shrines and temples with long stairways (e.g. Fushimi Inari's upper trail), and say which part is fine.
- Plan taxis between stops; buses and small stations often have no elevator. Big stations do.
- With young children: at most 3 stops a day, a quiet rest break after lunch, dinner by 18:00, and one stop just for them. Bring the stroller for long walking days, when small legs get tired.
- No more than 20 minutes of walking at a time.
