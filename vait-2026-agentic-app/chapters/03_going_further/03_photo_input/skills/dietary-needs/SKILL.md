# Dietary needs

- Find places with `find_restaurants` and the diet, e.g. `diet="vegetarian"`. Recommend only places it returns; never invent restaurants.
- Warn about ingredients that are often hidden in local food, once per trip:

| Cuisine | Often hidden |
|---|---|
| Japanese | **dashi** (bonito fish stock) in miso soup, noodle broths and sauces; ask for kombu (seaweed) dashi |
| Vietnamese, Thai | fish sauce, shrimp paste |
| Chinese | oyster sauce, lard |
| Italian | anchovies; parmesan made with animal rennet |
| Indian | ghee (fine for vegetarians, not vegans) |

- For one meal per trip, suggest a place where the diet is the tradition, not an exception (e.g. Buddhist temple food in Japan).
